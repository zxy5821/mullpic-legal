MullPic Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-11-06 · Applies to the Android app com.mullpic.app

1. What MullPic is

MullPic is a local photo-organizing tool. You review photos with swipes or buttons and decide to keep them or set them aside. Photos you set aside are moved only after you confirm Android's system deletion dialog. The app runs on your device and does not upload your photos.

2. Free plan and Premium

  • The free plan allows 50 decisions per local calendar day. Undo returns a decision. The smart scan is limited to the newest 1,000 photos and at most 10 groups per pass.
  • Premium removes both limits. It is sold as weekly, monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and as a one-time lifetime purchase. All products grant the same entitlement.
  • Prices, currencies and any free trial are shown by Google Play at purchase time and may differ by country. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the paid period.
  • The lifetime purchase is not a subscription and never renews. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy.
  • Restoring purchases uses the Google account that bought them; it works on any device signed in with that account.

3. Your responsibilities

  • You decide what to delete. Review the queue before confirming; MullPic never deletes without the system dialog.
  • Whether photos can be restored from system trash depends on your device, gallery app and Android version. MullPic does not guarantee a restore period and does not promise that storage is freed immediately.
  • Do not use the app to process media you have no right to modify.

4. Availability and changes

The app is provided as is. Duplicate, similar and blurry suggestions are heuristics and may miss photos or flag photos you want to keep. Features may change between versions; material changes to paid entitlements will be described in the release notes.

5. Privacy

See the Privacy Policy. Photos and decisions stay on your device; purchases are processed by Google Play.
