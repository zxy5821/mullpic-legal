MullPic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06 · Applies to the Android app com.mullpic.app

Short version

MullPic keeps your photos on your device. The app has no account, no cloud storage, no analytics and no advertising SDK, and it declares no internet permission. Your photos are never uploaded.

What the app accesses

  • Photos you choose or allow. With the system photo picker, only the photos you select. With photo-library permission, the images and their basic metadata (file name, size, album, added/taken date) so you can sort, group and delete them. Thumbnails are generated on the device for display and for local duplicate/similar/blur analysis.
  • Local records you create. Your decisions, daily free-decision count, settings and a small fingerprint signature per analyzed photo are stored inside the app's private storage on this device. They are not transmitted anywhere.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions and the one-time unlock are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details; MullPic only receives whether an entitlement is active and stores that flag locally. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google processes payments.

What the app does not do

  • No photo, thumbnail, file name, EXIF data or analysis result is uploaded.
  • No account, login or profile is created; no email address is required.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDK is included.
  • Photos are removed only after you confirm the system deletion dialog. Moving to system trash depends on your gallery and Android version; MullPic does not promise a specific restore period.

Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED (Android 13+) and legacy read access (Android 11–12): optional, requested only when you choose to scan your library, and revocable at any time in system settings.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: used by Google Play to process purchases. The app itself has no network permission.

Storage and deletion

All records live in the app's private storage and are removed when you clear them in Settings or uninstall the app. System trash is managed by Android and your gallery; empty it there if you want the space back.

Children

MullPic is a general-purpose photo organizer and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this URL with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@mullpic.example (replace with the support inbox before release).

MullPic 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-11-06 · 适用于 Android 应用 com.mullpic.app

简述：MullPic 把照片留在你的设备上。应用没有账号、没有云端存储、不接入统计或广告 SDK，也未声明联网权限，照片不会被上传。

应用会访问什么

  • 你选择或授权的照片。使用系统照片选择器时只读取你选中的照片；授权照片库后读取图片及基本元数据（文件名、大小、相册、添加/拍摄时间），用于整理、分组与删除。缩略图在设备上生成，用于显示和本地的重复/相似/模糊分析。
  • 你产生的本地记录。整理决定、每日免费次数、设置，以及每张已分析照片的小型指纹签名，保存在应用私有目录，不会外传。
  • 购买。订阅与一次性解锁由 Google Play 结算处理。支付信息由 Google 处理，MullPic 只收到权益是否有效的状态并保存在本机。

应用不会做什么

  • 不上传照片、缩略图、文件名、EXIF 或分析结果。
  • 不创建账号，不需要邮箱。
  • 不包含统计、崩溃上报或广告 SDK。
  • 只在你确认系统删除对话框后才移除照片；能否从回收站恢复取决于设备、图库应用与系统版本，应用不承诺固定恢复期限。

权限

  • 照片读取权限（Android 13+ 的 READ_MEDIA_IMAGES、READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED，以及 Android 11–12 的旧权限）：可选，只在你主动扫描照片库时请求，可随时在系统设置中撤销。
  • com.android.vending.BILLING：由 Google Play 用于处理购买；应用本身没有联网权限。

存储与删除

所有记录保存在应用私有存储，可在设置中清除，或卸载应用一并删除。系统回收站由 Android 与图库应用管理，需要回收空间请在系统图库中清空。

儿童

MullPic 是通用照片整理工具，不面向儿童。

变更

政策更新时会在本地址发布新版本并更新日期。
